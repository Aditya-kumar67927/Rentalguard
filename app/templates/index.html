Price anomaly engine

Robust peer statistics (median rent per sq.ft by locality and BHK) blended with a gradient-boosted regression model trained on 9,000+ Mumbai listings.

Text & NLP analysis

Detects advance-payment requests, "no viewing" claims, owner-abroad stories, urgency and unusual payment channels, with TF-IDF similarity to catch paraphrases.

Duplicate detection

MinHash LSH + TF-IDF cosine similarity finds near-identical listings, and flags copies that appear with a different price or in a different part of the city.

Contact patterns

Contacts are hashed, never displayed. Repetition across many localities and community reports are surfaced as patterns, not accusations.

Deposit analysis

Compares the deposit-to-rent ratio with typical Mumbai deposits (a median of about 4.4 months' rent in the reference data).

Machine-learning estimate

A gradient-boosting classifier combines weak signals. It is trained on documented synthetic labels and only adds up to 20 of the 100 points.

A risk assessment, not a verdict. RentalGuard highlights unusual characteristics that deserve further verification. A high score does not prove a listing is fraudulent, and a low score does not guarantee that it is safe.
